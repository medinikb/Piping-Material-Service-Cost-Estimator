# AGENTS.md

Instructions for future Codex work on this project:

- Preserve calculation accuracy.
- Do not change engineering formulas without explanation.
- Keep the tool static and GitHub Pages compatible.
- Avoid external paid dependencies.
- Keep UI simple, mobile friendly, and fast.
- Maintain audit trail and calculation transparency.
- Any future formula change must be documented in README and visible in the page audit trail.
- Preserve approved piping service-rate library rules: CS applies to Carbon Steel, SS applies to Austenitic Stainless Steel, and AS applies to Alloy Steel. Keep a visible CS fallback for Unclassified pipe, fitting, and flange rows only; never extrapolate rates, keep unsupported material or rate combinations marked Review, and keep service cost separate from material cost unless a documented scope change is approved.
